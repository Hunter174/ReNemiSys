# Development-only retreat requests (ReNemiSys #13 prototype; #15 certification OPEN)

**Not a group-contagion mechanic.** A retreating actor requests assistance from the nearest ally obstructing its outward path. That ally may consent to a short, game-authorized sidestep or refuse; when it refuses, the requester may take a separately collision-checked sideways counter-route or guard. A refusal does not magically revoke an independently legal route. At most two local hops propagate a *space request*, not another actor's fear or policy weights. No SaLMon/LLM call, network wait, persistent trait change, new epiphany or per-character model is involved. The existing v3 combat policy still proposes ordinary tactics; authored, inspectable recipient utility provides the snap response. Parameters in `retreat_coordination.gd` are untuned authored values; #14 owns any future offline search.

The request contains stable actor ID, recipient ID, current game revision, next-decision-tick expiry, and outward vector. Recipient decisions use its visible HP, current action/commitment, courage and distance to player as *inputs*, never modify them. A low-HP or tactically committed ally can refuse. A proposal to yield contains collision-checked targets for up to two allies; Godot checks the live actors, legal response, wall bounds and unique reservations **again**, then applies the entire plan or none. The temporary move ends after 0.36s or death/unload/revision invalidation. Godot physics resolves real `CharacterBody2D` collisions; there is no teleport through friends, enemies or walls. A stale/expired answer is rejected, leaving the requester with a legal guard. A contested helper's rejected reservation can instead produce an independently legal counter-route for the requester; proximity at the START of a route does not forbid motion strictly *away* from that contact. Swept corridors are checked against the player as well as allies, including when the target itself is clear; the game rechecks proposed recipient paths and disallows reassigning a still-yielding ally to a *different* requester. One requester can renew its own short-lived consent. A wall-blocked outward lane does not revoke an independent sideways exit. Request/response runs synchronously on the next 0.2s action update, not in an LLM queue; fast response is not guaranteed under arbitrarily blocking game code.

## Scoped #13 prototype completion: collision-checked recovery (`consent-recovery-dev-v2`)

A **game-owned** contact-recovery pass now runs identically with consent ON and OFF in the isolated 900–907 development arena. It acts only when Godot's normal `CharacterBody2D.move_and_slide` leaves a goblin pair penetrating by >1px. Up to two passes request at most **2px per actor per physics tick** of *physical* outward movement via `move_and_collide`; collisions with live bodies and walls can block that correction. It does **not** change anyone's action, courage, ambition, response or exit reservation, and it never teleports a helper. Unresolvable pairs remain counted rather than being hidden. Maximum *observed actual* cumulative per-actor correction on these opened starts: **1.53px off / 1.18px on**. This is collision recovery, **not implied ally consent**. The normal arena and the opened 610xx physics contract never enter this flag. No combat weights changed.

In separate Godot processes on the **same repeatedly opened development 900–907 seeds**, with recovery enabled in BOTH modes: consent OFF **772 hits / 4 goblin defeats / 0 pair-frame >1px residual goblin overlaps** (38 physical correction attempts); consent ON **776 / 4 / 0** (19 attempts). Maximum final sampled goblin penetration was **1.00px when rounded to hundredths** in either mode; the exact strict `>1px` test recorded zero. This does **not** establish collision safety on other crowds or improve group survival against a new opponent. Player/wall contact, denied corrections in tighter dogpiles, perceptual fairness and lasting main-game consequences need the new #15 gate. All 10 pure contract checks, 17 default-recovery Godot physics cases **and** 17 explicit legacy-collision fixtures passed, including rational refusal, walls, two independent and contested simultaneous requests, death/unload, and stale/expired replies. Accelerated headless outcomes are NOT rendered timing.
